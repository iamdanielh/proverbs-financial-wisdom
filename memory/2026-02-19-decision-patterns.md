## Pattern 1: Health Decisions — Self-Diagnostic → External Validation → Action

### The Holter Case Study (59K words, 130 messages)
**Trigger:** Symptoms + self-observation
> "Mido 170 peso 235 libras. Comer mucho arroz me sube el ritmo cardiaco"

**Self-Diagnostic Phase:**
- Self-identifies root cause: obesity + carb sensitivity
- Eliminates variables: "No tomo ningun medicamento"
- Seeks pattern confirmation: "Osea comer mucho carbohidrato me jodio?"

**Option Generation:**
- Weight loss as primary solution
- Medication (Metformina) consideration
- Dietary modifications
- Lifestyle changes

**Verification Pattern:**
- "Es mala la metformina?"
- "Es de venta libre?"
- "La necesito tomar?"

**Decision Criteria:**
- Return to "normal weight of 75 kilos" ← specific target
- Natural methods preferred over medication
- Medical accuracy prioritized ("Ensure to give an accurate answer")

**Insight:** Treats health as engineering problem — symptoms → diagnosis → solution options → verification → implementation

## Pattern 2: Financial Decisions — Scenario Modeling → Stress Testing → Execution

### The House Purchase (31K words, 62 messages)
**Opening:** Scenario-based hypothetical
> "I am paying 500 dollars in rent... how much house could I buy without having to spend more than 500 in expenses beyond equity building"

**Analysis Approach:**
- Anchors on current state ($500 rent)
- Seeks equivalence point (not "more")
- Factors in cash position ($100k)

**Complexity Layering:**
- Tax implications (401k, non-citizen status)
- Retirement strategy overlap
- Temporal trade-offs (10 years vs immediate)

**Critical Thinking Pattern:**
- Challenges conventional wisdom: "The capital is compounding in the house... I do not understand that narrative is like a lie"
- Stress-tests assumptions: "if I get a mortgage instead of paying cash I will be paying 6% interest on top of home owner expenses"
- Life constraints included: "We are living with my wife family I can't do this for 10 years"

**Decision Triggers:**
- Impatience with inefficiency
- Family constraints
- Mathematical optimization

**Insight:** Treats financial decisions as constrained optimization — current anchor, equivalence point, stress-test assumptions, factor life constraints

## Cross-Cutting Decision Architecture

### 1. Scenario-First Framing
All decisions start with "what if" or current-state anchoring:
- Health: "Mido 170 peso 235 libras"
- House: "I am paying 500 dollars in rent"
- Career: (implied current-state dissatisfaction)

### 2. Self-Diagnostic Prioritization
Attempts own analysis before accepting external input:
- Health: self-identifies carb sensitivity
- House: challenges "compounding in house" narrative
- Career: (implied self-awareness of gaps)

### 3. Multi-Layer Verification
Doesn't accept first answer:
- Health: 29 verification checks across conversation
- House: Challenges conventional wisdom explicitly
- Career: Tests answers against intended outcomes

### 4. Constraint Integration
Includes "soft" constraints in "hard" decisions:
- Health: prefers natural method
- House: "I can't do this for 10 years" — family integration
- Career: (citizenship status, retirement plans)

### 5. Natural Language Flexibility
Makes high-stakes decisions in both languages:
- Health: Spanish ("Osea comer mucho carbohidrato me jodio?")
- House: English (complex tax optimization)
- Career: Mixed ("Me ayudas a prepararme?")

### 6. Documentation-Oriented
Decisions become reference materials:
- Health: 59K-word Holter "Resumen"
- House: Calculation threads preserved
- Career: Resume/Cover letter iterations saved

## Decision Style Summary

| Stage | Dan's Approach | Pattern |
|-------|----------------|---------|
| **Trigger** | Self-observation / dissatisfaction | Notices, measures, articulates |
| **Diagnosis** | Self-analysis first | Attempts own framework |
| **Options** | Scenario-based exploration | "What if I..." / "How much..." |
| **Validation** | Multi-turn verification | "Is that right?" / "Does that make sense?" |
| **Constraints** | Hard + soft integration | Family, time, preference, citizenship |

## For Future Dan-Aware Systems

**When helping with decisions:**

1. **Start with current state anchoring** — "What are you paying/doing now?"
2. **Allow self-diagnostic space** — Let him articulate his own analysis first
3. **Present scenarios, not just answers** — "Option A... Option B..."
4. **Expect verification** — There will be "is that right?" follow-ups
5. **Surface constraints early** — He will integrate them anyway
6. **Connect to documentation** — Decision becomes a file
7. **Support bilingual processing** — Spanish is valid for high-stakes

**Trigger phrases for decision conversations:**
- "Mido..." / "Currently paying..." / "I am at..."
- "What if..." / "How much..." / "Should I..."
- "Does that make sense..." / "Is that right..."
- Family/time constraints mentioned
- Specific target numbers (75 kilos, $500, age 45)
