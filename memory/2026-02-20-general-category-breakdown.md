## Implication for Dan-Aware AI

### When Dan Opens With:
**Untitled / "New chat"**
→ Quick, direct answer. No iteration expected.

**"What is..." / "How do I..."**
→ Information request. Verify accuracy. He checks.

**"Make my day" / Random curiosity**
→ Entertainment request. Be creative. No follow-up expected.

**"My..." / Personal framing**
→ Context matters. Family, health, constraints.

**Spanish**
→ Personal or immediate. Respond in Spanish.
