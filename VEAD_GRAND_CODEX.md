### INFP Personality Type
- **Idealist & creative:** Seeks authentic meaning
- **Ego-driven yet empathetic:** Uses success/validation to fill void
- **Practical spirituality:** Post-conversion adopts non-dogmatic faith

### Background
- **Origin:** Colombian poverty, violent alcoholic father
- **Trauma:** Isolated from age 7, bullied for poverty
- **Influence:** Telenovelas → idealized rich men as safety
- **Religion:** Adventist upbringing — taught God as judge, not father

### Arc Stages
1. **Corrupt Ambition:** Success through bribes, adopts partner's arrogance
2. **BREAK:** Business collapse, flees, becomes nomad
3. **Disguised Material Search:** Travel content, "hacks" that hide emptiness
4. **CRISIS:** Loses girlfriend, poverty, sickness, abandonment
5. **REVELATION:** Hymn in Buddhist street (Ch 46) → Surrender (Ch 47)
6. **TRANSFORMATION:** Returns transformed, new purpose, preaching
