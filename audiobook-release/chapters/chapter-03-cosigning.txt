Chapter 3: The Snare of Co-Signing—Managing Financial Risk

Key Proverbs Verses

Proverbs 6, verses 1 through 3: "My son, if you have put up security for your neighbor, if you have given your pledge for a stranger, if you are snared in the words of your mouth, caught in the words of your mouth, then do this, my son, and save yourself."

Proverbs 11, verse 15: "He who puts up security for a stranger will surely suffer harm."

Proverbs 22, verse 26: "Be not one of those who give pledges, who put up security for debts."

Expanded Discussion

The language is physical and violent: trapped, ensnared, fallen into your neighbor's hands. These are not metaphors of minor inconvenience. They describe loss of freedom, vulnerability to others' decisions, and potential destruction.
