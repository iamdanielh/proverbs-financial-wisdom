💡 Structural Question: Where does Integrity fit?

Integrity/Business Ethics (23 verses) could be:

  • Standalone: As its own major section (currently 23 verses)
  • Foundation: Combined with Wisdom chapters (makes sense—can't build wealth without integrity first)
  • Distributed: Split across Earning (honest gain) and Protecting (avoiding ill-gotten wealth)

My recommendation: Make it Foundation Section 2—right after Wisdom. The sequence would be: Wisdom → Integrity → Earning → Managing → Giving → Protecting → Legacy.

This works because Proverbs 10-11 actually interweaves integrity warnings with diligence/wealth themes.
